# Evidence Integrity / 事实完整性

Use this reference whenever a resume or CV is rewritten, translated, shortened, or tailored. The goal is persuasive wording without changing the underlying facts.

## Non-negotiable boundary

Never invent, estimate, or silently strengthen any of the following:

- numbers, percentages, money, rankings, headcount, volume, frequency, or scale;
- employer, client, institution, product, project, location, job title, degree, certification, or award;
- dates, duration, sequence, employment status, or seniority;
- causality or business impact (for example, changing “associated with” into “caused”);
- ownership or scope (for example, changing “supported” into “led,” “contributed” into “built,” or a team result into an individual result).

A JD, O*NET profile, industry norm, sample resume, or model assumption is never evidence that the candidate personally did something. Do not infer proficiency from a tool name or infer results from a responsibility.

## Evidence labels

Label every material claim before using it:

- **Direct / 直接证据** — explicitly stated in the supplied CV, draft, portfolio, user message, or another candidate-provided source. It may be paraphrased faithfully without adding scope or certainty.
- **Transferable / 可迁移证据** — a documented competency demonstrated in a different role, domain, tool, or setting. Describe the actual experience and make its relevance clear; do not imply that the candidate performed the target job, used an unverified tool, or held equivalent seniority.
- **Missing / 缺失证据** — no supplied source supports the claim. Omit it from the final resume or ask for confirmation; never turn it into a resume fact.

If sources conflict, preserve both source spans, mark the claim unresolved, and ask the user. Do not choose the more impressive version.

## Claim check

For every new or materially changed statement, separate the claim into: actor, action, object, method/tool, organization/context, date/duration, scale, result, causal link, and ownership. Each included element must be supported by a candidate source.

Keep a compact working ledger when tailoring substantial content:

| Proposed claim | Candidate source + span | Label | Safe action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Faithful restatement | Exact field, bullet, or user message | Direct | Use |
| Same skill in another context | Exact field or bullet | Transferable | State the real context; connect relevance cautiously |
| Unsupported or ambiguous detail | None or conflicting spans | Missing | Omit or request confirmation |

Source spans should be specific enough to audit, such as `Experience > Company A > bullet 2` or the exact user message. Do not cite only “resume.”

## Confirmation protocol

Use the exact marker `[待确认：...]` for any unresolved fact, in either a Chinese or English draft. Ask one concrete question inside the marker, for example:

- `[待确认：该项目由你独立负责，还是与团队共同完成？]`
- `[待确认：Please confirm the 18% figure and its measurement period.]`

Keep markers outside polished final bullets when possible. Before export or submission, resolve every marker or remove the unsupported claim. Never treat silence as confirmation.

## Final integrity gate

Before returning a draft:

1. Compare every number, named entity, date, result, causal statement, and ownership verb with its source span.
2. Downgrade inflated verbs and certainty to match the evidence.
3. Report transferable evidence as transferable, not as direct experience.
4. List unresolved markers and missing evidence separately from the resume.
5. State that the draft is fact-checked only against the materials supplied, not independently verified.
